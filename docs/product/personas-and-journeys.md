# Personas and Journeys

Personas are deliberately spread across confidence, profession and device. Every major screen is reviewed against at least Imran, Grace and Sana before it is considered done.

## Personas

### Imran — delivery driver, 34
- **Device:** Android phone, occasionally a shared family laptop.
- **Goal:** Apply to a logistics company that asked for "a CV by email".
- **Has:** 9 years of driving, licence categories B and C1, clean record, knows the routes. Never written a CV.
- **Fears:** Looking unprofessional; long forms; English spelling.
- **Needs from us:** Few questions, big tap targets, examples in plain words, a one-page CV that looks serious.

### Grace — school teacher, 41
- **Device:** Laptop.
- **Goal:** Move to a better school; wants something "modern but not flashy, maybe blue".
- **Has:** Old Word CV from 2017, a teaching certificate, subject specialisms.
- **Fears:** Losing formatting; templates that look like a marketing flyer.
- **Needs:** Import or paste her old CV, choose an elegant look, tweak colour, export a two-page PDF that prints perfectly.

### Sana — fresh graduate, 22
- **Device:** Phone and laptop.
- **Goal:** First internship or graduate role.
- **Has:** Degree, a final-year project, part-time retail job, volunteering.
- **Fears:** "I have no experience."
- **Needs:** Encouragement; education-first layout; help turning projects and part-time work into strong lines without exaggeration.

### Daniel — senior software engineer, 37
- **Device:** Desktop, large monitor.
- **Goal:** Tailored CVs for several roles.
- **Has:** Long history, many technologies.
- **Needs:** Fast keyboard-friendly editing, multiple versions, job-description tailoring, ATS-safe output, fine control over spacing and sections.

### Amina — registered nurse, 29
- **Device:** Tablet and phone, often during breaks.
- **Goal:** Hospital role abroad.
- **Has:** Registration number, clinical areas, certifications (BLS/ACLS).
- **Needs:** Profession-specific sections (registration, clinical experience), save progress and come back later.

### Robert — operations director, 55
- **Device:** Laptop.
- **Goal:** Board-level and executive roles.
- **Needs:** Understated, executive-grade typography; achievements-led summary; confidence the document looks expensive.

## Core journeys

### J1. First CV from nothing (Imran, Sana) — Phase 3
1. Lands on home page → "Create my CV".
2. Chooses what they do from friendly cards (or types it: "I drive delivery vans").
3. Answers a short, profession-aware set of steps: contact → work → education → skills (+ profession extras like licences).
4. Sees their CV appear in a professional template chosen for their profession.
5. Optionally adjusts colour/template; downloads PDF.
**Success:** a one-page PDF in under 10 minutes, without creating an account.

### J2. Describe the CV I want (Grace) — Phase 4 (AI); Phase 3 ships the non-AI version
1. Types: "Modern professional CV for a school teacher, blue colours, clean and elegant."
2. Studio replies with its interpretation in plain words ("Elegant single-column layout, navy accents, serif headings") and applies it to her content.
3. She can say "a bit warmer" or pick from 2–3 alternatives.
**Phase 3 non-AI version:** style picker with descriptive cards ("Calm & classic", "Modern & clear", "Bold sidebar").

### J3. Improve my existing CV (Grace, Robert) — Phase 4 (wording), Phase 5 (import)
Paste or upload text → structured into the document → AI suggests clearer wording per section with accept/reject per suggestion.

### J4. Tailor to a job (Daniel) — Phase 5
Paste job ad → see what matches and what is missing → create a tailored version (master untouched) → review changes → export.

### J5. Come back later (Amina) — Phase 3 (same device), Phase 6 (any device with account)
Work autosaves locally; returning shows "Continue your CV". With an account, CVs sync across devices.

### J6. Cover letter for this job (Grace, Daniel) — Phase 7
From a CV, "Write a cover letter" → paste job → letter draft in matching design → edit → export / copy as email.

### J7. Portfolio page (Sana, Daniel) — Phase 9
Publish a simple public page from the CV with projects; shareable link.

## Accessibility and inclusion notes

- Write at roughly a 12-year-old reading level in all interface copy.
- Never require the words "ATS", "template", "prompt" or "section" to complete a task; use them only as secondary hints.
- Support long names, non-Latin names (rendered with appropriate fallback fonts), right-to-left content later (Phase 10+).
- Do not penalize short histories: a one-page, three-section CV is a complete CV.
